## The Trap: Why Home Care Destroys Festive Menswear

When fatigue kicks in late at night, many men make impulsive garment care decisions that permanently ruin expensive designer ethnic wear:

<Callout type="danger" title="Mistake 1: The Bucket Wash & Spin Cycle Disaster">
  Tossing raw silk, tussar silk, or structured Nehru jackets into a washing machine drum dissolves fabric sizing, wrinkles delicate armhole canvassing, and triggers irreversible shrinkage along neck bands.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Mistake 2: The Local Press-Wala's Scorching Flat Iron">
  Traditional street coal or heavy electric flat irons apply direct 200°C dry heat and crushing pressure. On velvet jackets or dark silk-blend kurtas, this permanently flattens the plush fiber pile, leaving shiny, glazed heat scorch marks that cannot be undone.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warning" title="Mistake 3: Trapped Sweat Salts in Synthetic Linings">
  Standard grocery store laundry detergents cannot break down the crystallized sodium, urea, and sebum oils trapped inside synthetic viscose and polyester inner linings. Over 24 hours, these salts attract moisture, break down thread integrity, and leave stale, sour odor halos.
</Callout>
