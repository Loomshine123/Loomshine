# Website Information Architecture — Loomshine

## 1. Route Map
- `/`: Primary Marketing Homepage
- `/services`: Comprehensive Service Details
- `/how-it-works`: Process & Care Standards
- `/pricing`: Transparent Rate Card
- `/about`: Brand Story & Craftsmanship
- `/business`: B2B Corporate & Hospitality Laundry
- `/track-order`: Live Order Status Tracking
- `/contact`: Service Coverage & Inquiries
- `/login`: Customer Account Access
- `/book-pickup`: Pickup Booking Flow

---

## 2. Homepage Section Ordering

1. **SECTION 01 — HERO**:
   - Eyebrow: `THE LOOMSHINE PROFESSIONAL GARMENT CARE`
   - Headline: `YOUR CLOTHES. OUR CRAFT.`
   - Supporting text: `Professional laundry and dry cleaning, carefully handled and delivered to your doorstep.`
   - Actions: `Book a Pickup` (Primary), `Explore Services` (Secondary)

   - Eyebrow: `LOOMSHINE LAUNDRY & DRY CLEANING`
