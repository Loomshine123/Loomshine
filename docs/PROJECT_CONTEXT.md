# Project Context — Loomshine

## 1. Brand Identity
- **Brand Name**: Loomshine
- **Business Focus**: Premium professional laundry, dry-cleaning, press & finish, and specialized shoe/bag garment-care service.
- **Visual Aesthetic**: Editorial, luxury, minimal, clean, dark navy and off-white contrast.

## 2. Customer Promise
"Professional garment care with doorstep pickup and delivery."

## 3. Primary Strategic Goals
1. **Generate Pickup Bookings**: Seamless online scheduling for doorstep pickup.
2. **Explain Services**: Detail specialized garment care capabilities across fabric types.
3. **Build Trust**: Establish authority through garment expertise, quality control checks, and clear process timelines.
4. **Communicate Premium Garment Care**: Educate customers on fabric-first care (wool, silk, linen, cotton, delicates).
5. **Show Transparent Pricing**: Clear pricing tiers per kg, per garment, or per item.
6. **Enable Order Tracking**: Allow customers to track active order status (Picked Up -> Cleaning -> Quality Check -> Out for Delivery -> Delivered).
7. **Promote Business Services**: Tailored B2B solutions for hotels, spas, salons, restaurants, and corporate clients.

## 4. Primary Services
1. **Laundry**: Everyday washing, drying, and folding.
2. **Dry Cleaning**: Professional eco-friendly solvent care for suits, dresses, and delicate fabrics.
3. **Press & Finish**: Crisp steam pressing and finishing for crisp shirts and trousers.
4. **Shoe & Bag Care**: Specialized cleaning, restoration, and polishing for luxury footwear and leather accessories.

## 5. Target Business Customers (B2B)
- Hotels & Boutique Stays
- Salons & Spas
- Corporate Offices
- Restaurants & Fine Dining
- Hospitality Businesses

## 6. Tone & Voice Guidelines
- **Tone**: Premium, Clear, Professional, Confident, Human, Concise.
- **Avoid**: Generic marketing clichés, cheap sales language, excessive exclamation marks, fake claim statements, and artificial urgency.
